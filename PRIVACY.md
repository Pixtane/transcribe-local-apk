# Holos — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 29 October 2026_

Holos ("the app") is a text-to-speech app developed by Pixtane. This policy explains what
happens to your data when you use it. The short version: **nothing leaves your phone.**

## Data we collect

**None.** Holos has no user accounts, no advertising, no analytics, no crash-reporting service
and no servers of its own. We do not collect, store, sell or share any personal data.

## Your texts, documents and audio

Text you type or paste, files you import (TXT, PDF, EPUB, MOBI…) and the speech generated from
them are processed **entirely on your device** and stored only in the app's private storage.
Deleting a recording, or uninstalling the app, removes them.

## Internet access

The app uses the internet only to download voice models when you ask it to, from
huggingface.co and github.com (the sherpa-onnx project's releases). These requests contain no
personal data beyond what any download sends (such as your IP address, which those services
may log under their own privacy policies). Text-to-speech itself works fully offline.

## Permissions

- **Notifications**: show playback controls and generation progress.
- **Foreground service / wake lock**: keep playing audio and generating speech while the screen is off.
- **Internet**: download voice models.

## Purchases

Holos is sold through Google Play. Payment is handled entirely by Google; we receive no payment
card or billing details. See Google's privacy policy for how Google processes purchase data.

## Children

Holos is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from anyone.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address with a new date.

## Contact

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/Pixtane/transcribe-local-apk/issues

# Holos — Політика конфіденційності

**Holos не збирає жодних даних.** Немає облікових записів, реклами, аналітики чи власних серверів.
Ваші тексти, імпортовані файли та згенероване аудіо обробляються й зберігаються лише на вашому
телефоні. Інтернет використовується тільки для завантаження голосових моделей (huggingface.co,
github.com) на ваш запит. Оплата відбувається через Google Play; ми не отримуємо платіжних даних.
